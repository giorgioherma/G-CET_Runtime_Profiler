## Blocking source and safety findings

- **The original observer serves more than the ActionRouter.** User-supplied 0-Engine `init.lua` has one `Observe('PlayerPuppet','OnAction')` callback that executes `ActionRouter.Dispatch(...)` and may subsequently call **`Events.PlayerAction:trigger(action)`** when playing and outside menus. Dynamic legacy event listeners, original order and wake paths must be inspected against the **currently installed** source.
- Other mods register their own `PlayerPuppet::OnAction` callbacks or original fallbacks; no **global** OnAction suppression, native engine-method short circuit, or arbitrary callback reordering is permissible.
- In native CET source, examine the real `FunctionOverride` / `RealRunScriptFunction` subscriber dispatch and argument conversion sequence. An action CName must be read safely **before** per-observer Lua argument construction for a pre-marshalling saving to exist; a method hash, RTTI guess or Lua-side skip after conversion is insufficient. Unknown action/type, subscriber identity, snapshot, session, or observer state must **deliver the original callback**.
